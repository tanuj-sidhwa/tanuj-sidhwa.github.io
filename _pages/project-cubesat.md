## Engineering work

- Contributed to chassis modelling, component integration, and technical documentation.
- Performed structural, modal, and thermal finite-element studies to assess mechanical response.
- Evaluated design safety margins and examined how structural assumptions influence predicted behaviour.
- Coordinated team responsibilities and documented the resulting design decisions.

## Outcome

The project received a **bronze distinction** at IITISoC 2024. Analysis supported the design assessment; it should not be interpreted as full flight qualification or physical environmental testing.
