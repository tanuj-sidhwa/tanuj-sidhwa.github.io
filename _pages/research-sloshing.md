<p class="research-detail__lede">Liquid motion in a partially filled container is a canonical unsteady multiphase-flow problem, with practical implications for transport, motion control, and spacecraft fluid systems. I am using OpenFOAM to study transient free-surface behaviour.</p>

## Research objective

Develop a reliable computational workflow for sloshing cases and assess the physical response of the liquid interface under prescribed tank motion or excitation conditions.

## Computational approach

- Set up and operate OpenFOAM cases for free-surface flow.
- Examine numerical configuration, boundary conditions, time-step selection, and interface tracking.
- Investigate transient liquid-interface displacement and pressure response, subject to the chosen excitation and tank geometry.
- Use post-processing and visualization to interpret the simulated free-surface dynamics.

## Current status

This is an ongoing numerical research project. Quantitative results, convergence studies, and any validation comparisons will be included here after they are completed and reviewed.
